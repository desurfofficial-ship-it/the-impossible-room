The Arches
BLUE rotates gravity +90° — you fall onto Wall A. RED rotates −90° — Wall B. YELLOW inverts it — the ceiling becomes your floor. GREEN resets to the room floor. Arches stack: on Wall A, a blue arch sends you to the ceiling. Watch the gravity compass, bottom-left. If you fall into the void you awake at the green arch.
