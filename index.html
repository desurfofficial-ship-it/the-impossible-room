Arches rotate which way is down. Shapes mark each plane so color is never the only cue.

BLUEWALL A REDWALL B YELLOWCEILING GREENFLOOR

Arches stack. On Wall A, blue sends you to the ceiling. Watch the gravity compass. Fall into the void and you wake at the green arch.

Every arch multiplies your score — up to ×12. The chain decays in twelve seconds. Death breaks it. A shard briefly shields the decay.

The room does not explain the solution. Reading the space is the puzzle.

Chambers I–V are lessons. A live checklist tracks each skill. The floor catches Academy falls so you can learn without soft-locking.

VI–XV are machines. XVI+ are endless vaults. The Daily Vault is a shared seed for one day.
